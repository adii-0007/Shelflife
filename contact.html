Common questions

Quick answers so you don't have to wait for a reply.

How soon before expiry should I list my product?

You can list anytime, but products within 15 days of expiry get automatically flagged for discount pricing and appear on our "Ending Soon" section for maximum visibility.

How is the discount price decided?

You set your own discount when listing. We recommend 30—50% off based on days remaining — the closer to expiry, the higher the recommended discount to sell faster.

Is there a fee to list products?

Listing is free for verified local sellers. We only take a small commission on completed sales, so you only pay when you actually sell.

What happens if a product isn't sold before expiry?

Unsold listings are automatically removed 2 days before the expiry date to keep the platform trustworthy for buyers.

How do buyers know the product isn't already expired?

Every listing shows the exact expiry date and days remaining. We verify seller-submitted dates and remove any inaccurate listings.

Can I list products from multiple store branches?

Yes. ShelfLife supports multi-branch sellers. Reach out via the form above and select "Other" as the category, and mention your branches in the message.
